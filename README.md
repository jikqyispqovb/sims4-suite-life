## Overview

Suite Life is a The Sims 4 mod that introduces boutique hotel and rental property management, letting Sims own and operate a hotel, take guest reservations, manage housekeeping and front desk staff, respond to guest requests, and build a star-rated hospitality reputation. It transforms the Sims 4 into a property management simulation for players who want a business-focused play style.

Whether you're a newcomer just getting started or a seasoned pro looking for advanced tools, **The Sims 4 Suite Life** delivers the features you need in a clean, intuitive package.

## Key Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Hotel Ownership** | Build, furnish, and operate multi-room hotels as a business venture |
| **Guest System** | NPC guests check in, make requests, and leave reviews affecting your rating |
| **Staff Management** | Hire and direct housekeeping, concierge, and maintenance employees |
| **Star Rating** | Hotel reputation system driven by guest satisfaction and amenity quality |
